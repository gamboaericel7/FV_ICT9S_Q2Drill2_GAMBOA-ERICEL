OBMC stands for Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center, which is a school that focuses on students’ learning and development. It uses the Montessori method, which helps students learn independently and at their own pace. The school teaches important values like respect, responsibility, and discipline. It also encourages students to be creative, confident, and helpful to others. Overall, OBMC aims to prepare students to become responsible and successful individuals in the future.
